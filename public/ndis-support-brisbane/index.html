What We Offer

NDIS Services Available in Brisbane

All supports are funded through your NDIS plan and delivered by disability support workers based in Brisbane. Pricing aligns with the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements.

Personal Care in Brisbane

Support with dressing, grooming, hygiene, and daily personal tasks. Your Brisbane support worker will provide consistent, dignified care that fits around your routine and preferences.

Community Access & Participation

Get out and experience everything Brisbane has to offer. We support participants to visit South Bank, local markets, the Botanic Gardens, community hubs, sporting events, and activities of your choosing.

Transport Services – Brisbane

Safe, reliable transport to medical appointments, therapies, work, and social activities across Brisbane's northside, southside, inner city, east, and western suburbs.

Skill Development & Capacity Building

Build real independence with practical life skills – cooking, budgeting, using public transport, communication, and more. We work alongside you to achieve the goals set out in your NDIS plan.

Daily Personal Activities

Ongoing support for everyday living – morning routines, meal preparation, medication reminders, and tasks that help you stay independent at home and out in the Brisbane community.

Household Task Assistance

Brisbane disability support workers can assist with cleaning, laundry, meal prep, and home maintenance so you can focus on living your life rather than managing your household.
