About This Service

What Is NDIS Personal Care on the Gold Coast?

NDIS personal care on the Gold Coast refers to hands-on support with the personal tasks of daily life that a person with disability finds difficult or impossible to do independently. This includes showering, bathing, dressing, grooming, continence management, and moving safely around the home. Personal care is one of the most common supports funded under an NDIS plan, and it plays a central role in helping participants maintain their independence, health, and dignity.

At Shine Disability Care, our Gold Coast personal care workers are carefully matched to each participant based on gender preferences, cultural background, language, and schedule. We understand that personal care is intimate and trust-based – which is why we prioritise sending the same support worker to every session rather than rotating through a roster of unfamiliar faces.

Whether you need support for a short morning routine or extended assistance throughout the day, our Gold Coast team can build a schedule around your life. We service all Gold Coast suburbs including Southport, Robina, Burleigh Heads, Coomera, Palm Beach, Varsity Lakes, and Coolangatta. All personal care services are funded through the Core Supports budget in your NDIS plan.

For a full overview of all disability support services available on the Gold Coast, visit our Services page.

Is This Service Right for Me?

Who Benefits from NDIS Personal Care Support?

Personal care support is suitable for participants of all ages and disability types. Here are some signs that NDIS personal care on the Gold Coast may be the right fit for you or your family member:

You find it difficult, painful, or unsafe to complete showering, dressing, or grooming tasks on your own due to a physical, neurological, or cognitive disability.

A family member or informal carer is currently providing personal care and needs respite or additional support to manage the role sustainably.

You have personal care included in your NDIS plan under Core Supports – Assistance with Daily Life and want to choose a registered Gold Coast provider.

You want to maintain a consistent routine and personal hygiene standard that supports your health, self-esteem, and community participation.
