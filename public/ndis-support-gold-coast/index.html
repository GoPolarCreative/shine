What We Offer

NDIS Services Available on the Gold Coast

All supports are funded through your NDIS plan and delivered by disability support workers based on the Gold Coast. Pricing aligns with the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements.

Personal Care on the Gold Coast

Support with dressing, grooming, hygiene, and daily personal tasks. Your support worker will provide consistent, dignified care that fits your routine – not the other way around.

Community Access & Participation

Get out and enjoy everything the Gold Coast has to offer. We support participants to attend beaches, markets, community events, social outings, and activities of your choosing.

Transport Services – Gold Coast

Safe, reliable transport to medical appointments, therapies, work, and social activities across Southport, Robina, Burleigh Heads, and all Gold Coast suburbs.

Skill Development & Capacity Building

Build real independence with practical life skills – cooking, budgeting, public transport, communication, and more. We work alongside you to achieve goals your NDIS plan sets out.

Daily Personal Activities

Ongoing support for everyday living – morning routines, meal preparation, medication reminders, and any tasks that help you stay independent at home and in your community.

Household Task Assistance

Disability support workers can assist with cleaning, laundry, meal prep, and home maintenance so you can focus on living your life rather than managing your household.

All services are delivered by experienced disability support workers and funded through your NDIS plan under Core Supports or Capacity Building budgets.
