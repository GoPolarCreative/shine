What is this service?

What Is NDIS Daily Activities Support on the Gold Coast?

NDIS daily activities support – formally called Assistance with Daily Life under Core Supports – covers the ongoing, recurring daily tasks that a person with disability needs assistance with to live at home. This includes morning routines like showering and dressing, meal preparation, medication prompting, moving around the home safely, and personal hygiene support.

What makes daily activities support distinct from one-off personal care is that it's built into a regular schedule. Participants often have daily activities scheduled Monday to Friday or every morning to maintain a consistent routine. On the Gold Coast, our workers cover all suburbs and can be scheduled to arrive at the same time each day, creating the predictability that matters so much for your wellbeing.

Daily activities support is often the backbone of a participant's support schedule – it's the support that keeps everything else running smoothly. Without it, other activities like community access, transport, or skill development become harder to access. That's why Shine prioritises consistency: same worker, same time, every visit. Routine matters, and we build ours around you.

This service suits participants of all ages – children, adults, and older participants with disability. It can also include support for complex needs such as medication management, catheter or bowel care, and PEG feeding if your participant's plan provides for this. Ready to explore how daily activities support could enhance your independence? Browse all our services or get in touch with our Gold Coast team today.

Who Benefits from NDIS Daily Activities Support?

If you're living on the Gold Coast with a disability and struggling to manage daily tasks independently, or if an informal carer is stretched thin trying to help you, this service could make a real difference. Here's who we typically work with.

  • You need regular daily assistance with morning or evening routines – showering, dressing, meal preparation, medication prompting – and want a consistent support worker who knows your preferences.

  • You are living at home on the Gold Coast with a physical, intellectual, or neurological disability and need structured daily support to maintain your health, safety, and independence.

  • Your NDIS plan includes Core Supports – Assistance with Daily Life funding that you want to use with a registered, reliable Gold Coast provider.

  • You or a family member currently manages daily tasks but the arrangement is unsustainable and you need a provider who can take over part or all of the daily routine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NDIS daily activities support on the Gold Coast

What does NDIS daily activities support include on the Gold Coast? +
NDIS daily activities support on the Gold Coast covers the ongoing tasks of daily life that a participant needs assistance with at home: morning routines (showering, dressing, grooming), meal preparation, medication prompting, moving safely around the home, and personal hygiene. For participants with more complex needs, daily activities support can also include continence care, catheter management, bowel care, and PEG (gastrostomy) feeding where this is included in the participant's plan.
How is daily activities support different from personal care? +
The distinction is largely one of frequency and scheduling. Personal care refers to the tasks themselves (showering, dressing, grooming). Daily activities is the broader support category that covers all ongoing daily living tasks, including personal care. In practice, both are funded under Core Supports – Assistance with Daily Life. The key difference is that daily activities support is typically scheduled as a regular daily or near-daily service, whereas personal care can be more flexible or session-based.
Can I have daily activities support every morning on the Gold Coast? +
Yes. Many of our Gold Coast participants have daily morning support scheduled 5–7 days a week. We work to ensure the same support worker arrives at the same time each morning so your routine is predictable and consistent. We understand that for many participants, the reliability of their morning support has a direct impact on the rest of their day.
What happens if my regular support worker is unavailable? +
We try to give as much notice as possible when your usual worker is unavailable. We will always attempt to source a replacement worker who you have met before or who has been briefed on your routine and preferences. If no suitable replacement is available, we will contact you to discuss options. We do not send unfamiliar workers without your knowledge.
