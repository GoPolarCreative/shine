FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Shine Disability Care.

We provide personal care assistance, community access support, transportation services, skill development, daily personal activities, and household task assistance. Our services are tailored to your individual NDIS plan goals. Contact us to discuss whether we can support your specific needs.
Yes, all our pricing aligns with the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. We review our rates whenever the NDIS updates its guidelines and you will never be charged more than the published NDIS price limits.
We follow the NDIS cancellation policy. Short-notice cancellations (under 2 business days) may be charged at the agreed support rate. We always outline cancellation terms clearly in your Service Agreement before services commence.
Provider travel covers the time and costs for a support worker to travel to you. Any charges are calculated in line with NDIS Pricing Arrangements and will be discussed with you before being applied. All travel costs are documented clearly in your Service Agreement.
Yes, we work with all NDIS funding management types. Whether you are agency-managed, plan-managed, or self-managed, our team will guide you through the process. Just let us know your funding type when you reach out.
Your Service Agreement outlines the supports we will provide, agreed pricing, the support schedule, cancellation terms, and both parties' rights and responsibilities. We walk every participant through the agreement before signing and are happy to answer any questions.
We match each participant with a consistent support team so you build real relationships with those supporting you. Our coordinators conduct regular check-ins, respond promptly to queries, and keep families and support coordinators updated at all times.
